# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three distinct readers arrive at this site:

- **Technical recruiters and hiring managers** scanning for signal in 30 seconds: Do the projects show depth? Is this person senior? Can they communicate clearly? They decide whether to pass this on or set up a call.
- **Senior engineers and research collaborators** reading for 3–5 minutes: They want to understand *how* Mohammed thinks, not just what he shipped. They read the project stories, look for intellectual honesty, and assess whether this is someone worth working with.
- **SAiR community and students** arriving via Telegram or GitHub: They're looking for the org link, curriculum, or Mohammed's teaching work. They're the beneficiaries of the mission, not evaluators of his career.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio is Mohammed Sedeg's primary professional surface. It must do three things simultaneously: establish technical credibility with rigorous engineering evidence, project a genuine human presence that matches the SAiR founding story, and convert readers into collaborators, employers, or community members. Success looks like a hiring manager forwarding it, a researcher replying to a cold email, or a student finding the Telegram link.

## Brand Personality

Expert and human. Not "friendly AI engineer." Not "serious researcher." Both at once. The same person who writes pure-PyTorch autograd engines from scratch also founded a free AI org in Sudan because knowledge shouldn't be gated. Intellectual honesty is the throughline: the project stories say what was hard, what broke, what the actual constraint was. No self-promotion inflation. Evidence only.

Three-word summary: **Rigorous. Personal. Earned.**

## Anti-references

- Dark dev portfolio template: black background, neon green or purple, terminal rain, particle effects, floating code snippets. Mohammed's work is too substantial to hide behind aesthetic theater.
- Generic SaaS landing page: gradient text, hero-metric stat blocks, feature grids with identical icon+text cards, AI-generated copy feel.
- Overdesigned creative agency: scroll-jacking, WebGL heroes, loading spinners, motion for motion's sake.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over assertion.** Every design decision earns its place the same way the projects do: by doing a real job. No decorative flourishes that don't serve the reader.
2. **Precision is respect.** The audience is technical. Sloppy spacing, inconsistent hierarchy, or generic components signals the same carelessness as sloppy code. Every detail is deliberate.
3. **Warmth through content, not chrome.** The human dimension comes from the writing, the Sudan flag, the SAiR story — not from rounded corners, gradient overlays, or particle effects.
4. **Show the thinking, not just the outcome.** Project stories reveal reasoning: what was hard, what the real constraint was, what changed from prototype to production. The design should give these stories room to breathe.
5. **One site, two scripts.** Arabic and English are equally first-class. RTL layout, Cairo typeface, and direction-aware spacing are not afterthoughts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum; AA on color contrast for all body text and interactive elements.
- Full keyboard navigation. Focus states visible and on-brand.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` already wired; maintain it across all new animation additions.
- RTL support for Arabic: critical, not optional.
- The audience skews technical (desktop, modern browsers) but mobile must be fully functional, not just tolerable.
